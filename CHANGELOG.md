# Changelog

## v0.2
Fixes from player feedback on v0.1.
- **Move cards** redrawn so they can be told apart at a glance: T L S F in the colours of the
  original kanji, and the OTHER, finisher and double-team cards as white symbols (O, star, +) on
  solid colours. The finisher and double-team cards were still in Japanese.
- **Naming wrestlers and promotions**: name entry's kanji page is now PAIRS, two-letter pieces in
  the same narrow letters as the rest of the English (names up to 20 letters).
- **Wrestler data screen** in Exhibition, League and Tournament: the move type buttons (THR LCK
  STK FLY OTH) were still in Japanese.
- **Text still in the original wide font** is now in the English letters: the foreign
  promotions (IWWF, WWCA, EWA, GWA, AAC, JWI, WARS), also in the alliance menu; Lv1-8,
  1P/2P/3P/COM, AP, STF/DDT, IN/OUT, ON/OFF and the "&" between tag champions.
- **Move names**: Uranage (was "Throw"); the Northern Lights Suplex finisher is "N.Lights Suplex"
  (was "Hokuto-plex").
- **New promoter game**: the CPU promotions' names line up against PRO.
- The promoter mode's three button codes are confirmed in game ([docs/HIDDEN_CONTENT.md](docs/HIDDEN_CONTENT.md)).
- Saves from v0.1 should carry over (every letter kept its place in the font; not yet tested
  with a real save). In a promoter game started on v0.1 the alliance menu keeps the wide
  promotion names (the game copies them when the game starts).
- Not changed: numbers still use the game's wide digits.

## v0.1
- First public release. The whole game in English: Exhibition, Promoter, League and Tournament
  modes, the system menu, match commentary, events, scouting talks, profiles and the ending, plus
  the labels, buttons and banners drawn as graphics.
- Tested in Mesen 2: every mode played through; the promoter mode's endings, year-end rankings,
  Ex Tag League and rare events reached in the emulator.
